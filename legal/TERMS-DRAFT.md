Do not publish this file as final terms. Supply the seller's legal identity and contact, supported platforms, exact paid feature list, license duration/device policy, price/currency/tax treatment, refund and support process, renewal policy, and applicable dispute terms with appropriate review.

The development build is unsigned and not certified as fully feature-equivalent to Snagit, ShareX, PicPick, or Snipping Tool. Do not sell the evaluation build using those claims. Include a supported-capabilities matrix and validation evidence for the build offered to customers.
